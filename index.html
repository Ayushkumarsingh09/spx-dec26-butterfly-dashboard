Method

Prices are Black-76. The forward (7,848.27) and time to expiry (68.19 days; the grid header says 67d and gives no forward) are solved so that Black-76 at Bloomberg’s mid IVs reproduces the quoted mids (worst error 0.37 pts); the rate is the header’s 4.33%. Today’s smile is a smoothing spline through the out-of-the-money mid IVs: puts below the forward and calls at or above it (dots on the smile chart), each point weighted by the tightness of its quote.

With the base smile set to Flat vol, every strike uses the one vol you choose (default 12.87%, the ATM vol) for both today and the scenario, so the sliders show how a flat-vol fly reacts; the dots on the smile chart stay the market quotes.

Scenario smile at strike K, with x = 100·ln(K / Fscenario) (% moneyness): σ = σtoday(K′) + shift + skew·x + curvature·x² + put-wing·x² (x < 0) + call-wing·x² (x > 0). Sticky strike uses K′ = K; sticky moneyness uses K′ = K · Ftoday / Fscenario, so the smile travels with the forward.

Calls and puts share one smile, the out-of-the-money composite, so a put has the same IV as the call at its strike. With equal-width wings the put–call parity term is linear in K and cancels in a 1 : −2 : 1 fly, so a put fly costs exactly what the call fly costs. The “Fly built from” switch changes which legs are priced; the ATM legs table shows both side by side, and the dots on the option-premiums chart are the quoted mids.

      Monthly review

      One column for every session in the selected month. The first line in a cell is the level. The line under it is the change from the previous session. Green is an increase and red is a decrease.

      Spot is the underlying. ATM vol is 50Δ implied vol. Risk reversal is put IV minus call IV. Put skew is put IV minus ATM, and call skew is ATM minus call IV. Kurtosis is put IV plus call IV minus twice ATM. Wing kurtosis is the 10Δ wings minus the 25Δ wings. Fly IV is the local butterfly: the average of the two neighbouring deltas minus the centre. Fly premium is that same butterfly on the option premium.

      Month charts

      ATM offsets

      Implied vol and premium are at each strike relative to that session’s ATM. A fly of width 100 at ATM −200 is long ATM −300, short two ATM −200, and long ATM −100. Fly premium is that package price, taken from the quoted premium. Fly IV is the average of the wing vols minus the body. Widths of 50, 25, and 10 keep the same body, interpolate the wing vol between the quoted strikes, and reprice each leg. The line under a level is the change from the previous session: green is an increase, red is a decrease.
